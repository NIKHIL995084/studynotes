Newton's Laws of Motion: Inertia, Force & Dynamics

Subject: Physics • Level: College Study Notes Academic Engine

📌 High-Yield Core Concepts

  • 1st Law (Law of Inertia): An object persists in its state of rest or uniform rectilinear motion unless acted upon by a net external resultant force.
  • 2nd Law (Fundamental Law): Rate of change of linear momentum is proportional to applied force: F = dp/dt = m·a.
  • 3rd Law (Action-Reaction): For every action force, there exists an equal in magnitude and opposite in direction reaction collinear force (F_AB = -F_BA).

💡 Essential Formulas & SI Units

Force: F = m · a (Newton, N = kg·m/s²) | Momentum: p = m · v | Impulse: J = ∫F dt = Δp

🎯 High-Yield Exam Tip & Common Pitfall

Common Mistake: Action and reaction forces NEVER act on the same body! They act on two distinct objects, which is why they never cancel each other.

🗺️ Concept Flowchart & Process Map

Applied Force ➔ Momentum Changes (dp/dt) ➔ Acceleration (a = F/m) ➔ Reaction Force (-F)

Thermodynamics & Energy Conservation

1st Law: Internal energy change ΔU = Q - W. Energy cannot be created or destroyed.
2nd Law: Total entropy of an isolated system always increases (ΔS ≥ 0). Natural processes are irreversible.
Carnot Engine: Maximum theoretical efficiency η = 1 - (T_Cold / T_Hot) (T in Kelvin).
Formula: η = 1 - (T_C / T_H) | ΔU = Q - W
